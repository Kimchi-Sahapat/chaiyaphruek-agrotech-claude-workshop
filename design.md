# ชัยพฤกษ์ อโกรเทค — Corporate Identity Guide

## Brand Personality

Warm, energetic, generous, trustworthy. A community hub — not just a shop. Visuals feel like a celebration the farmer is invited to, not a corporate announcement. Real people, real prizes, real expertise.

## Color System

### Primary Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brand Green | Lime Green | `#4DB748` | Primary background, dominant color across all materials |
| Brand Navy | Deep Navy Blue | `#1B3A8C` | Brand name "ชัยพฤกษ์", major headlines, numbered elements |
| Brand Red | Festival Red | `#E8323C` | Brand name "อโกรเทค", urgency, CTAs, activity numbers |
| Gold / Yellow | Harvest Gold | `#FFD700` | Prize callouts, "นาทีทอง", key highlight words |
| White | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary text on green, stroke outlines on large text |

### Secondary Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dark Green | Forest Green | `#1E7B34` | Footer backgrounds, borders, text on light areas |
| Cream / Light Yellow | Soft Cream | `#FFFDE0` | Footer strips, panel backgrounds, logo badges |
| Light Green | Radial Ray Green | `#7DC75A` | Sunburst/ray pattern on green backgrounds |

### Brand Name Color Rule — CRITICAL

The brand name **always splits into two colors**:

> **"ชัยพฤกษ์"** = Navy Blue (`#1B3A8C`)
> **"อโกรเทค"** = Red (`#E8323C`)

This applies wherever the brand name appears as text in a design — headers, banners, posters. Never render the full name in a single color.

### Color Rules

- **Green is always dominant.** Every piece should have green as the primary background.
- **Navy blue for headline text** on green backgrounds (not just white) — gives contrast and brand consistency.
- **Red signals action or urgency** — activity numbers, CTAs, "อโกรเทค", deadlines.
- **Gold elevates the single most important word** — prize, headline, key benefit.
- **White is used as stroke/outline** on large navy or red text to make it pop off green.
- **Never use dark/black backgrounds.**

## Typography

### Thai Font Stack

| Weight | Role | Example Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Black / ExtraBold | Hero headlines | "นาทีทอง", "จับไข่ลุ้นทอง", campaign names |
| Bold | Subheadlines, product names | Section titles, expert names, dates |
| Regular / Medium | Body copy, contact info | Descriptions, footer info |

### Recommended Fonts

- **Primary:** Kanit (ExtraBold / Bold / Regular) — rounds well, legible at all sizes on mobile
- **Fallback:** Noto Sans Thai — system-safe, consistent rendering
- **Never use:** Serif fonts, script fonts, fonts with low mobile legibility

### Type Hierarchy (Square Post 1080×1080)

| Level | Size (approx) | Weight | Color |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero text | 90–130px | ExtraBold | White or Gold |
| Subhead | 50–70px | Bold | White |
| Supporting text | 30–45px | Regular/Medium | White |
| Footer / contact | 22–28px | Regular | White |

### Rules

- Text must be readable at thumbnail size (200×200px). If it can't be read at that size, increase weight or size.
- Key benefit words are always larger than surrounding text — never uniform size across a headline.
- Line spacing: 1.1–1.2 for headlines, 1.4 for body.

## Logo

### Mark Description

Circular badge — white background inside a thick **red ring** border, with text curved around the top arc:

- **Text "ชัยพฤกษ์ อโกรเทค"** — curved along the top of the circle, **Navy Blue (`#1B3A8C`) ทั้งหมด**, font rounded bold Thai (style คล้าย Kanit Black)
- **3 green trees** (`#4DB748`) — flat lollipop/rounded top shape, center tree tallest, two shorter trees flanking; thick tapering trunk
- **5 wavy navy blue lines** (`#1B3A8C`) — water waves in the middle zone, white gaps between each line
- **Yellow/gold semi-circle** (`#FFD700`) — ground/earth mound at the base
- **Red ring border** (`#E8323C`) — thick outer circle, no fill

The mark represents the full agricultural cycle: land → water → growth.

### ข้อสำคัญ — ข้อความในโลโก้ vs บนสื่อ

| บริบท | "ชัยพฤกษ์" | "อโกรเทค" |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ในโลโก้ circular mark** | Navy Blue | Navy Blue (เหมือนกัน) |
| **ข้อความ standalone บนสื่อ** | Navy Blue | Red |

โลโก้ mark ใช้สีน้ำเงินล้วน — การแยก navy/red ใช้เฉพาะข้อความที่พิมพ์แยกจาก mark บนสื่อต่างๆ

### Placement

- **Always top-left หรือ top-center** บน social media posts
- Never stretch, recolor, หรือ rotate
- Minimum clear space: เท่ากับความสูงของ mark ทุกด้าน

### On backgrounds

| Background | Version |
| --- | --- |
| Green | Full-color mark (ข้อความ navy) |
| White / Light | Full-color version |
| Dark | White outline version — ทุก element กลายเป็นขาว |

## Content Types & Visual Treatment

### 1. Promotion / Giveaway Posts
- Background: Bright lime green (`#4DB748`)
- Hero element: Prize image (floating/prominent)
- Headline: White + Gold highlight word
- Urgency element: Red badge or sticker ("สุดพิเศษ!!", dates)
- Energy level: High — use sparkle/star decorative elements

### 2. Expert / Knowledge Posts
- Background: Green with real photo of expert or farm
- Photo: Real person, warm expression, in-field or natural setting
- Text overlay: Semi-transparent dark green panel
- Tone: Authoritative but approachable
- "หมอทุเรียน" framing for durian expertise positioning

### 3. Event / Festival Posts
- Feature the mascot (green hat character)
- Date and time prominently displayed
- Use the word "เฟสติวัล" or "งาน" as hero text
- Include vendor logos grid if applicable

### 4. Product / Brands Posts
- Logo grid layout for multi-brand posts
- Clean white or light background for logo visibility
- "จากกว่า 25 บริษัทชั้นนำ" framing — emphasize curation, not just availability

### 5. Real People / Testimonial Photos
- Minimal text overlay — let the photo breathe
- Warm, candid expressions preferred over posed
- Green uniform/clothing is a natural brand signal
- Caption-led: let the story be in the caption, not on the image

## Mascot

- Green wide-brim hat, white uniform with brand logo
- Used for: events, festivals, friendly announcements
- Never used for: serious/expert content, complaints, negative contexts
- Tone: Cheerful, welcoming, community-focused

|---|---|
| Real farmers, real fields | Stock photography of generic farming |
| Warm natural light | Cold studio lighting |
| Genuine smiles, interaction | Stiff posed group photos |
| Green uniforms visible | Unbranded clothing in hero shots |
| Eastern Thailand landscape | Generic/non-local scenery |
| Durian trees, fruit, farms | Unrelated crops or foreign environments |

## Decorative Elements

- **Sunburst / radial rays:** Lighter green rays on the primary green background — signature pattern for event and promotion posts. Adds depth without changing the dominant color.
- **Red ribbon banners:** Curved red ribbon shape used for dates, event names, announcements — always white text on red.
- **Numbered red circles:** Bold white number inside a red circle — used for activity lists (1, 2, 3 activities). Creates clear visual hierarchy.
- **Stars / sparkles:** Used in promotion posts for energy and celebration.
- **Floating product images:** Prizes and products shown at slight angles with drop shadow.
- **Gold coin / egg motifs:** Tied to prize/lucky draw campaigns.
- **Rounded rectangles:** All badge/label overlays use rounded corners — never sharp.
- **White pill badge:** Logo + brand name inside a white rounded rectangle — used on banners and wide-format materials.
- **Gradient backgrounds:** Green-to-yellow-green gradient (left to right) used on horizontal banners.
- **Circular icon cards:** Grey circles with icon + label text below — used for services/activities lineup at the bottom of event posters.

## Brand Voice in Visuals

The visual always matches the copy tone:

| Copy tone | Visual feel |
|---|---|
| Promotional / Giveaway | Bright, busy, gold, stars, high energy |
| Educational / Expert | Clean, real photo, readable, calm green |
| Community / Event | Mascot, crowd, warm photos, celebratory |
| Urgency / Deadline | Red accent, countdown, bold dates |

---

## What to Avoid

- Dark/black backgrounds
- Generic clipart or non-Thai agricultural imagery
- Too many fonts in one piece (max 2 weights, same font family)
- Text covering faces in real photos
- Inconsistent footer — every post must have contact info
- Posting without the logo/header strip
- Using competitor brand colors (avoid pure blue, avoid purple)
